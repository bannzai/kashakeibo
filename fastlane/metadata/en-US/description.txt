Kashakeibo is an AI-powered budget app inspired by the Japanese kakeibo method. Just snap a photo or take a screenshot, and your expenses are recorded.

Snap a paper receipt. Screenshot your credit card statement or online shopping history. AI reads the amount, date, and store name, and turns them into budget entries.

■ No bank linking. Nothing to break, nothing to worry about.
Kashakeibo does not connect to your bank accounts. No broken integrations, no credentials to hand over. If you can see it on your screen, you can capture it — card statements, QR payment history, anything.

■ Features
- Receipt scanning: snap a paper receipt and AI creates the entry
- Screenshot import: turn card statements and purchase histories into entries
- Manual entry: quickly log cash spending
- Duplicate detection: catches double counting between receipts and card statements
- Linked source images: jump back to the original image anytime to verify or fix AI results
- Exclude from totals: keep entries out of your calculations individually
- Monthly summary: income, spending, and category breakdown at a glance

■ Premium
The free plan includes a monthly allowance of AI scans. Premium unlocks unlimited scans and full history.
