# Privacy Policy

bannzai (the "Provider") establishes this Privacy Policy (the "Policy") regarding the handling of user information, including personal information, in the Kashakeibo service (the "Service") provided by the Provider.

## User Information We Collect and How We Collect It

In this Policy, "User Information" means information that identifies users, activity history on communication services, and other information generated or stored in connection with users or their devices that the Provider collects under this Policy.

### Information You Provide Through the Service

- Images that you capture or upload, including photographs of receipts and screenshots of websites or application screens. These images may contain financial transaction information, such as credit card statements and purchase histories.
- Transaction details generated from image analysis or entered through input forms or other methods specified by the Provider, including amounts, dates, merchant names, and categories.

### Information Provided by Other Services

If you authorize the Service to connect to another service, such as your Apple ID or Google Account, we collect the following information from that service according to the authorization you grant:

- The identifier you use with that service.
- Other information that you permit the service to disclose under its privacy settings.

### Information Collected by the Provider

The Provider may collect information about how you access and use the Service, including:

- Referrer information.
- IP address.
- Server access logs.

## Purposes of Use

The Provider uses User Information for the following purposes:

- To extract transaction details from images you capture or upload, including through AI image analysis, and to record and display those details as a household budget.
- To accept registrations, verify identity, authenticate users, store user settings, calculate payments, and otherwise provide, maintain, protect, and improve the Service.
- To measure traffic and user activity.
- To provide notices about the Service and respond to inquiries.
- To address conduct that violates the Provider's terms, policies, or other rules concerning the Service.
- To notify users of changes to the Provider's terms, policies, or other rules concerning the Service.

## External Services

The Service uses the following external services. Information sent to each service is handled under that company's privacy policy.

- **Firebase (Google LLC):** Used for authentication, database storage of transaction details, and usage analytics through Google Analytics for Firebase. https://policies.google.com/privacy?hl=en
- **Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc.):** Used to store and deliver images you capture or upload. Access to images is limited to the authenticated user who owns them. https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/
- **AI image analysis (Google LLC / Gemini API):** Images you capture or upload are sent to Google's AI analysis service to extract transaction details. Analysis is performed for each request, and the image and analysis result are not stored permanently by the analysis service.
- **RevenueCat, Inc.:** Used to manage paid-service purchase status. https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

## Notice, Consent, and Requests to Stop Use

You may request that the Provider stop collecting or using all or part of your personal information through the settings specified by the Service. The Provider will promptly stop such use in accordance with its procedures. Some information is necessary to provide the Service; for that information, collection or use can be stopped only when you close your account through the method specified by the Provider.

## Disclosure to Third Parties

The Provider will not disclose personal information included in User Information to a third party, including a party located outside Japan, without your prior consent, except in the following cases:

- When handling of all or part of the personal information is outsourced to the extent necessary to achieve the purposes of use.
- When personal information is transferred as part of a merger or other business succession.
- When personal information is provided to a partner or information-collection module provider under terms or policies to which you have consented.
- When cooperation with a national or local government agency, or a party entrusted by such an agency, is required for duties prescribed by law and obtaining your consent may impede those duties.
- When otherwise permitted under Japan's Act on the Protection of Personal Information or other applicable laws and regulations.

## Disclosure of Personal Information

When you request disclosure of personal information under Japan's Act on the Protection of Personal Information, the Provider will confirm that the request is from you and disclose the information without delay. If the information does not exist, the Provider will notify you. This does not apply when the Provider is not legally obligated to disclose the information. A fee of JPY 1,000 is charged for each disclosure request.

## Correction, Suspension of Use, and Deletion

1. If you request correction because your personal information is inaccurate, or request suspension of use because it is being handled beyond the previously disclosed purposes or was collected by deception or other improper means, the Provider will confirm that the request is from you, promptly conduct the necessary investigation, take action based on the result, and notify you. If the Provider decides not to make a correction or suspend use, it will notify you of that decision.
2. If you request deletion of your personal information and the Provider determines that it is required to comply, the Provider will confirm that the request is from you, delete the information, and notify you.
3. The preceding provisions do not apply when the Provider is not obligated to correct, suspend use of, or delete the information under Japan's Act on the Protection of Personal Information or other applicable laws and regulations.
